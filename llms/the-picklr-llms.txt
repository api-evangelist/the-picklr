# The Picklr
> The Picklr is the largest indoor pickleball club franchise in North America, offering unlimited membership-based court access, professional coaching academies, leagues, tournaments, and events at locations nationwide. Founded in 2021 in Utah by Jorge Barragan and Austin Wood, The Picklr has grown to 80+ locations with 400+ franchise licenses signed across the U.S.

The Picklr operates premium indoor pickleball facilities designed for players of all ages and skill levels. Members enjoy unlimited court reservations, clinics, open play, leagues, and tournaments with a single membership that works at every location nationwide. The company also offers franchise opportunities for entrepreneurs looking to enter the fastest-growing sport in America.
